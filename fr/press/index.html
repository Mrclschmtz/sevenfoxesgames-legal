QUIZERRA KIDS

A quiz game for children aged 6 to 12: no ads, no tracking, no subscription.

Télécharger dans l’App Store
Gratuit · iPhone & iPad

Short description

Quizerra Kids is a quiz game for iPhone and iPad. Children answer as many questions as they can in two minutes, earn stickers for correct answers and stick them into a sticker book. The game has 800 kid-friendly questions in 10 categories and two age levels (ages 6 to 8 with read-aloud, ages 9 to 12), in German, English, Spanish and French. Four categories are free forever, more can be unlocked with one-time purchases, never with a subscription. The app has no ads, no tracking and no account.

Facts at a glance

  • Platform: iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later), works completely offline
  • Price: free; four categories free forever, more at €0.99 each or all for €4.99 (one-time purchases, no subscription)
  • Age: children aged 6 to 12; age rating 4+, Kids category on the App Store
  • Languages: German, English, Spanish, French
  • Content: 800 questions, 10 categories, 2 age levels, sticker book, daily challenge, statistics
  • Privacy: App Store privacy label: “Data Not Collected”. No ads, no analytics, no account
  • Released: October 5, 2026
  • Publisher: SevenFoxes Games, Marcel Krause

What parents want to know

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no account, no sign-up
  • All purchases and the only external link sit behind a parental gate (a small multiplication task)
  • One-time purchases instead of a subscription: bought is bought for good
  • Mistakes do not hurt: Ella the owl kindly shows the right answer, with no time-pressure alarms and no punishment
  • The read-aloud feature (ages 6 to 8) runs on the device and helps early readers play on their own
