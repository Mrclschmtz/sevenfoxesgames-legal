The studio

Small, independent, built by hand

SevenFoxes Games is a one-person studio. Every question in our apps is written by hand and checked in three languages – not translated, but phrased the way you would actually ask it in each language.

We build the games we like to play ourselves: quick to start, no sign-up, no waiting, and no tricks designed to keep you hooked longer than you want to be.

No account, no servers

Your progress stays on your device. We run no servers of our own and collect nothing.

Three languages, one catalog

English, German and Spanish – every question written and checked separately in each language.

Ad-free for kids

Quizerra Kids has no ads and no tracking. Anything that costs money sits behind a parental gate.
