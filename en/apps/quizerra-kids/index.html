For parents

So you can leave your child alone with it

Quizerra Kids was built from the start so that it collects, stores or transmits no personal data:

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools
  • No account, no sign-up – the app works completely offline
  • All purchases and external links sit behind a parental gate (a maths problem)
  • One-time purchases instead of a subscription: bought means bought forever, on all the family's devices

Two age levels

The right questions from the first launch

On first launch Ella asks how old you are and picks the right questions.

  • Ages 6 to 8

    Simple questions plus a read-aloud button, so early readers can play on their own.

  • Ages 9 to 12

    Trickier questions packed with fun facts to amaze you.

The sticker book

Collect, stick, rearrange

Answer questions correctly to earn stickers – five per category, each matching its theme: a cheetah, a T. rex, a rocket, a rainbow and many more. Stick them into the album and move them freely around the page. Every category has its own lovingly painted album page.

Four categories are free forever (Body & Senses, Tech & Vehicles, Food & Drink, Numbers & Puzzles), plus the daily challenge with questions from every category. Additional categories can be unlocked individually – or all at once, including every category we add in the future.
