BUMBLOSSOM

A bee puzzle full of blossoms

Every flower bed is a picture made of buds. Your bees circle it and pollinate the buds of their color – until a tulip, a hedgehog or a clownfish is in full bloom. Sounds simple, and gets trickier with every garden.

How to play

Bud by bud to the picture

  1. Tap a beehive – the front bee takes off and circles the bed.
  2. It pollinates every bud of its color it can reach from outside until its pollen runs out.
  3. If nothing fits right now, it waits on a parking spot. Once all spots are full, things get tight.
  4. When everything has bloomed, the picture is pressed and goes into your herbarium.

What makes Bumblossom special

  • Herbarium

    Every bed you finish is pressed and gets its place in the herbarium – 400 pictures to collect.

  • Helpers for tricky moments

    Watering can, bee swap, undo and an extra bee. When you are stuck, the rainbow bee pollinates any color.

  • Lucky buds

    Three buds with helpers and honey drops are waiting every day – the third one always brings a helper.

  • Totally relaxed

    No timer, no account, plays offline. Never any ads while you play a bed.

  • Gardener’s license

    A one-time purchase removes all ads and gives you three free helpers in every bed. Family Sharing included.

  • Made with love

    A hand-drawn look, one piece of music per garden and bees that cheer for every blossom.
